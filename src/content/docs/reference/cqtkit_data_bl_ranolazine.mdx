## Description

Baseline data for ranolazine C-QT study







## Format

## `cqtkit_data_bl_ranolazine`

A data frame with 132 rows and 17 columns:
- **ID**: Unique subject identifier
- **SEX**: Sex either M (Male) or F (Female)
- **AGE**: Age in years at screening
- **HGHT**: Height in cm at screening
- **WGHT**: Weight in kg at screening
- **RACE**: Race as provided
- **ETHNIC**: Ethnicity as provided
- **VISIT**: Visit code
- **TRTG**: Treatment Group (Drug or Placebo)
- **DOSE**: numeric value of dose
- **DOSEU**: Units of DOSE
- **TPT**: Timepoint of collection relative to first dose (h)
- **RR**: RR before dose (ms)
- **QT**: QT before dose (ms)
- **HR**: HR before dose (bpm)
- **QTCB**: Bazett's corrected QT before dose (ms)
- **QTCF**: Fridericia's corrected QT before dose (ms)
